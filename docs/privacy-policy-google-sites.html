Rooko Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Rooko helps you understand activity in Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts. This policy explains what the Android app uses, where it is kept, when the app connects to a network, and how to manage your information.

Information stored on your device

Rooko stores the following in Android's private app storage:

  • Your selected apps, appearance choice, consent, and counter settings.
  • Instagram creator handles and hashtags you choose to save, kept in Rooko's private app storage so you can reopen those profiles or searches. Rooko does not send them to its servers, analytics, or counting records.
  • Combined and per-app daily counts, hourly count totals, daily coverage information, and approximate daily observed-feed time.
  • Individual accepted-count times for up to seven days; older event times are removed while daily totals remain.
  • Detailed coverage-gap records for up to 30 days.
  • Legacy correction records, if present from migration, including the date, app, count difference, reason code, and creation time. They are not displayed or included in the current total.
  • Onboarding answers as a temporary local draft, cleared when onboarding is complete.
  • A cached signed detector-compatibility list, which contains app-version rules and no personal usage records.

Daily totals, hourly aggregates, coverage summaries, observed-time aggregates, legacy correction records, creator handles, saved hashtags, and settings remain on the device until you clear Rooko's app storage or uninstall Rooko. In-app export and deletion are not currently available. To clear local data, open Android Settings, choose Apps, choose Rooko, open Storage, and select Clear storage. Android backup settings may restore eligible app data.

Accessibility and counting

Counting starts only after you review the in-app disclosure, give affirmative consent, and enable Android Accessibility. Rooko processes only the selected Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts apps. Installed app versions are checked on your device. For some detection paths, Rooko may briefly read bounded visible labels and immediately hash them in memory for duplicate handling. Raw labels are not saved. Rooko does not save messages, images, audio, video, screenshots, or full Accessibility trees. You can turn off Accessibility access at any time in Android Settings.

Observed time

The approximate daily time total is built from short checks while a selected, recognized feed is in the foreground. It does not establish whether a video played or whether you watched it attentively. Long gaps and known interruptions are excluded. This measurement does not require overlay access.

Floating counter

The floating counter is optional and requires separate Android Display over other apps access. It shows Rook and your shared daily count over a recognized selected feed. It does not capture the screen or save feed content. Turn it off in Rooko or revoke overlay access in Android Settings.

Detector compatibility checks

Rooko may download a signed detector-compatibility list over HTTPS. That request does not include your selected apps, installed app versions, counts, or observed-time records. The external registry host and your network provider may receive your IP address, request time, and standard connection metadata. We have not verified the registry host's log-retention period.

App analytics and crash reports

Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics start automatically when Rooko opens on Android. This collection is separate from the Accessibility permission. Google Firebase may receive automatic app and session events, such as first opens and app use, and Rooko's parameter-free rooko_app_open event. It may also receive app-instance or installation identifiers, app version, device and operating-system details, and standard connection information such as an IP address. Crash reports may include exception messages and stack traces.

Rooko does not set a Firebase user ID or add your selected apps, counts, observed-time totals, Accessibility signals or content, feed labels, item IDs, screenshots, or Help & Feedback summaries to Firebase events, custom keys, or logs. Automatic Android Activity screen reporting and advertising ID collection are disabled. Rooko has no account, advertising, purchases, or usage-sync service. Your counts and observed-time totals are not uploaded to Rooko or sold.

Rooko currently has no in-app switch for Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics. Uninstalling Rooko stops future collection from that installation. Clearing app storage or uninstalling does not erase records already received by Google. Google's Firebase retention and data-handling terms apply; see Firebase privacy and security. For questions or a data request, use the contact address below. Rooko has no account linking an email address to a Firebase installation identifier, so a specific Firebase record may not be identifiable from an email alone.

Help and feedback

Troubleshooting details stay on your device unless you review a diagnostic summary and choose to copy it. Feature Request and Support Email actions open your email app; Rooko does not send a message or a diagnostic summary automatically. Information you choose to send by email is handled by your email provider and the support recipient.

Children

Rooko is not designed for children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information to the app, contact us so we can address the concern.

Changes to this policy

This policy may change when the app's data handling changes. The latest revision date appears at the top of this page. If a change affects why Accessibility is used, Rooko will request renewed in-app consent before using the updated purpose.
