# Rooko — Google Play “About this app”

## Short description

See your Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts habits with Rooko.

## Full description

How many Reels and Shorts did you scroll through today? Rooko helps you see the pattern behind the number.

Rooko is an Android companion for Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts. With your permission, it recognizes supported feeds and adds the items it detects to one daily count. You can follow that count on Today, explore it in Progress, and keep it in sight with an optional floating counter.

Your count, in one place

• See one combined daily total for Reels and Shorts, with separate Instagram and YouTube counts beneath it.
• Meet Rook, a small brain companion whose expression changes with your daily count.
• Choose which of the two apps to include. Rooko detects their installed versions automatically.

Look back with Progress

In Day view, see when counts were recorded on a 24-hour chart. Select an hour to read its count and, for recent recorded items, the individual counted times. The page also shows each app's share of the total and an approximate daily measure of time when a recognized feed was observed in the foreground.

In Week view, see the seven daily totals together. Explore your highest recorded day and open any date for its Day detail. When the underlying records are complete, Rooko can show a week-to-week comparison and a weekly observed-feed-time total. Missing records are shown as unknown rather than treated as zero.

Keep Rook nearby while you scroll

The optional floating counter shows the same combined total over a recognized Reels or Shorts feed. Move it or dismiss it whenever you want. This feature needs Android's separate “Display over other apps” permission; counting and Progress do not require the floating counter.

Your setup, your choice

Rooko explains why it needs Android Accessibility and asks for your consent before opening system settings. You can change your app selection in Rooko Settings and revoke access in Android Settings. Choose System, Light, Dark, or AMOLED appearance. Help & Feedback offers counting troubleshooting and a way to contact support. No account is required for local use, and all app features are free.

What the numbers mean

Counts are estimates, not an exact record of every item you watched. App updates, device behavior, or unavailable feed signals can cause missed or repeated items. Observed feed time measures only intervals when Rooko recognizes a selected feed in the foreground; it is approximate and does not establish video playback or attention. Some older or incomplete days may have no hourly or time detail.

Privacy

Rooko stores your count history and daily observed-feed-time totals locally. It does not save feed videos, images, audio, or readable feed text. The app uses Firebase for technical analytics and crash reports and checks a signed detector registry online. Read the full Privacy Policy: https://sites.google.com/view/rooko/home
